# 12 March, evening

Moved the 15:00 call with the supplier to next week. Sent an apology; the figures were not ready.

All morning I kept expecting that if I pushed on price, they would start looking for another buyer and nine years would be gone in one call.

Heavy and restless all afternoon. Relieved for about an hour after sending the message. Then I tidied the workshop instead of opening the cost sheets.

Next time: decide the number before the call.
